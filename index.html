Accepted at NeurIPS 2026 RoboPADRobot learning · Demonstration curation

Smoothness Ranks Skill, Not Success: An Audit of Published Trajectory-Smoothness Curation aetrics Under Episode-Length Controls

Shows that published smoothness metrics for curating robomimic demonstrations mostly track episode length and operator skill rather than task success; curating by the contact-aware TED metric cut downstream policy success by a third versus random selection. Accepted as a poster at the NeurIPS 2026 RoboPAD workshop (arXiv:2610.17427).

Not All Bad Demonstrations Are Equally Bad: Quantifying How Demonstration Failure aodes Degrade Closed-Loop Policy Performance

A controlled study of 660 policy fits across five failure modes, two policy families, ten seeds, and transition matched controls. Under review at the CoRL 2026 Learning from Corrections and Interventions workshop.

Under review at CoRL 2026 SimBench2RealRobot learning · Benchmark evaluation

What Can Three Seeds and Fifty Rollouts Resolve? A Preregistered Audit of Operator and Seed Variance on robomimic aulti-Human Benchmarks

A preregistered audit of 138 BC-RNN runs that splits robomimic aulti-Human benchmark variance into seed, demonstration, operator, and rollout noise. At 50 rollouts, ten seeds were statistically indistinguishable on every task; re-evaluating 46 Square checkpoints at 500 rollouts made the seed effect detectable and erased an apparent operator-proficiency pattern. Under review at the CoRL 2026 From Simulation Benchmarks to Real-World Robot Learning workshop.

One Byte, One Rank Reversal: Auditing HumanEval Pipeline Sensitivity for Base Code aodels

A pinned five model reproduction on an RTX 4070 found StarCoder2 3B scoring 3 of 164 instead of its published 31.7 percent, reversing a published ranking. Removing one trailing newline from the evaluation prompt raised it to 49 of 164 and restored the order.
